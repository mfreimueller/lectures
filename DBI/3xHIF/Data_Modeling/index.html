# Topic Description
00 Group Work Intro Course roadmap, grading, informal intro to data modeling
01 First Steps From a prose specification to plain tables, primary and foreign keys
02 ERM Fundamentals Chen notation: entity, relationship, attribute, keys, cardinality
03 1:n Chains Chaining 1:n relationships, composite/multivalued attributes, avoiding NULLs
04 n:m & Recursive Relationships n:m relationships with attributes, recursive (self-referencing) relationships
05 IS-A & Min-Max Notation Generalization/specialization, total/partial, disjoint, (min,max) cardinality
06 Roles & Multi-Participant Relationships Roles, time-boxed relationship attributes, ternary relationships
07 Weak Entities Partial keys, identifying relationships, composite keys
08 Real-World Application Applying the full notation toolkit to reverse-engineer a real website
09 Historization Valid-time vs. transaction-time, From-To pattern vs. Log pattern
10 Associative Entities & 3-Layer Architecture Associative entity sets, recursive relationships, ANSI/SPARC architecture
11 Transformation: ERM → RM The standard algorithm for mapping an ERM onto relational tables
12 Transformation: EER → RM Mapping weak entities, associative entity sets, and ternary relationships
13 IS-A Mapping Strategies Identity, Rollup, and Rolldown mapping strategies compared
